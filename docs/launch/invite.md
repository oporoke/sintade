# Invitation text for the first ten developer users

Send by hand, in English or Swahili as suits the person. Replace the bracketed parts.

---

**Subject:** Try Sintade: record your screen, share a link in seconds

Hi [name],

You record bug reports and walkthroughs for your team, so I'd like your honest opinion of Sintade,
a screen recorder we are building in Tanzania. It lives in the browser, uploads while you record,
and gives you a link seconds after you press Stop.

To try it:

1. Go to **[https://production origin]** and sign up (a minute; confirm the email we send).
2. Click **New recording**, choose a screen or window (add your microphone if you like), press
   Start, talk, press Stop. The link is copied for you; paste it to a colleague.
3. Optional: the Chrome/Edge extension records any tab in three clicks and draws click highlights.
   Until it is in the stores, install it from **[link to the extension zip]**: unzip, open
   `chrome://extensions`, turn on Developer mode, **Load unpacked**, choose the folder.

Good to know: the free plan gives 50 recordings of up to 10 minutes. Safari can watch but not
record yet. If your tab dies mid-recording, reopen Sintade and it offers to finish uploading.

What I'd love to hear, in any form: Did the link arrive fast? Did anything confuse you or fail?
What did you want to do that you couldn't? Reply to this message or write to [support contact].

Thank you,
[name]

---

## Three questions to ask after a week

1. How many recordings did you make, and how many did someone else watch?
2. What was the one thing that annoyed you most?
3. Would you use it instead of what you use today? What would make that a yes?
